A FEW GOOD THINGS TO KNOW

Before you dive in.

What files can I bring?

A UTF-8 CSV with a header row, or an .xlsx workbook up to 10 MB. Excel uploads support up to 10 sheets, with 100,000 rows and 100 columns per dataset. Older .xls and macro .xlsm files are not supported.

Can I change my data here?

The studio is read-only. Browse, search, sort and ask questions without editing the original cells.

How are answers calculated?

Gemini translates your question into a query plan, and pandas calculates the result from the selected dataset. You can inspect the query behind an answer.

Will my uploads stay here?

Uploaded datasets stay in the running backend’s memory. They are removed when the backend restarts. Questions and dataset schema are sent to the configured Gemini service.
